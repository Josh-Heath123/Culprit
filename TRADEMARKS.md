# Trademarks

[`docs/brand/wordmark.svg`](docs/brand/wordmark.svg)) are unregistered
trademarks of Joshua Heath. They are not registered in any country.

The licences in [`LICENSING.md`](LICENSING.md) cover the code and documents.
They do not grant any right to the name or the mark; this file says how they
may be used.

## Fine without asking

- Saying what Culprit is, writing about it, comparing it, teaching with it.
- Linking to this repository, or to a release of it, by name.
- Saying that your own work runs on, integrates with or is compatible with
  Culprit, when it is true, in a way that does not suggest it is Culprit or
  is endorsed by its owner.
- Running an unmodified Culprit and calling it Culprit.

## Use another name and mark

- **A fork,** or any modified version you distribute.
- **A hosted service** that runs a modified version.

Both may say, truthfully, that they are based on Culprit (for example "based
on Culprit"), in plain text, without the mark. The licences' own terms still
apply: under the AGPL, a modified version run as a network service offers its
users its source.
